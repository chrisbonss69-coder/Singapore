Controls and sources

Click the scene or use → or Page Down to advance. Use ← or Page Up to return. Space pauses. F opens fullscreen. R restarts. H opens or closes this panel.

On scene 2, the heart, book and renewable-energy model appear in sequence. Advance after the environment line. The NUS campus and WING have separate cues. The Toulouse–Singapore journey starts on your cue. Footage is local and silent.
